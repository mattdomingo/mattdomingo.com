Switchback Supper Privacy Policy

Switchback Supper works offline. The game does not collect personal data, use analytics or advertising, or send gameplay information to the developer or third parties.

When tilt steering is enabled, the game reads the phone’s motion during play to steer the skis. Motion readings are processed on the phone and are not saved.

Scores, run dates, distances, results, difficulty, and steering preferences are stored locally. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes its local game data.

For privacy or support questions, email matthew.domingo2022@gmail.com. If you contact the developer by email, your email address and the information you choose to share are used to respond to your request.
